A letter, from your Bubu
My Mahnoor,

It's me. Bubu. Bachaa. Your Boiboi. I answer to every one of them, because every one of them came from you.

I looked for a gift big enough for your 17th birthday, and I could not find one in any shop. So I made you a sky. I found the moon that carries your name, and the stars that carry your names, and I put them all in one night, just for you.

I picked every photo and every video of you myself, and watched each one longer than I should have, smiling like a fool at my screen. It is not perfect. But you were in my head the whole time, and that is the only way I know how to love: with everything I have.

I cannot promise you a perfect life. I can promise you someone who tries. Someone who shows up, who is soft with you on your hard days, who is proud of you out loud, and who remembers the small things.

The moon was full the night you were born. I think it was only practice. Ever since, it has been trying to be as bright as you.

If this were the only birthday I ever got to wish you on, I would still never run out of words. I would thank you for being born, for being exactly the way you are, and for letting me love you. You are the reason ordinary days feel like celebrations to me.

I am not wishing you a perfect year today. I am wishing you a year where you feel as loved as you truly are. I would give anything for you to see yourself the way I see you, just for one day. You would cry a little, and then you would smile, and then you would forgive every mirror that ever lied to you.

Happy Birthday, my Betu, my Gudiya, my Sindhu, my Lyra, my Lil Bee, my Love. Stay happy, stay kind, stay exactly you. I love you. Always.

Forever yours,
Karan
